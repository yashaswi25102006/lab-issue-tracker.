Project Overview & Team

About LabFix Portal

An automated, paperless lab infrastructure maintenance and ticket resolution system built to eliminate academic lab downtime across MG-001 to MG-405.

The Problem We Faced

During computer laboratory sessions, students frequently encounter hardware failures—missing mice, non-responsive keyboards, or blue-screen boot loops. Manually notifying lab in-charges or writing paper complaints takes valuable practical time and often goes untracked.

Our Proposed Solution

LabFix bridges the gap between students, lab technicians, and department heads. Featuring an interactive visual seat layout across all 9 MG labs, students can see green (working) and red (broken) PCs, cancel issues, and watch systems turn back to green once repaired.
